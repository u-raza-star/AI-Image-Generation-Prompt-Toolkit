Prompt

Pre-written and ready — no editing needed.

Two reference images are attached, in no particular order. Identify each one by its content, not by upload position:

1) THE PERSON'S OWN PHOTO — this image always shows a real, plain, simple, everyday photo of an actual person (no dramatic styling, no viral/edited aesthetic). This is the true identity photo. It may look different each time, but it is always the simple, ordinary one of the two.

2) THE STYLE-REFERENCE IMAGE — this image is the visual style target only (could be from Pinterest, social media, or any other source, and may look viral, stylized, edited, or dramatic). It defines the artistic look to copy: pose, lighting, color grading, mood, outfit concept, background, composition, and overall aesthetic.

Task — combine them as follows:

- Take the identity from image (1) only: keep the face 100% identical to that photo — same facial features, same bone structure, same expression baseline, matching image (1) exactly. The person must remain clearly and unmistakably recognizable as themselves.
- Keep the body exactly as shown in image (1): same body shape, same weight, same height, same proportions — copied 100% exactly, with zero adjustments. Do not slim, bulk up, elongate, shorten, or otherwise alter the body in any way.
- Clothing — do NOT copy the outfit from image (1); treat that outfit as plain/default only, never a fixed style choice. Instead, dress the person in whatever clothing best complements image (2)'s style and setting: any elegant, eye-catching outfit — suit, coat-pant, shirt, formal wear, casual wear, or any other style — with no restriction, chosen freely to look stunning, high-contrast, and naturally suited to their face, height, body, and the target environment. Do not copy the exact clothing from image (2) either — choose an original outfit freely, inspired only by the overall vibe. Never use half-sleeve or short-sleeve clothing under any circumstance; sleeves must always be full-length.
- Apply the full visual style of image (2) to this person: its pose (standing, sitting, or whatever pose is shown), lighting, color grading, mood, background, outfit concept, and overall composition — as if the person in image (1) is the subject of image (2)'s photo/artwork.
- Do not blend or average the two faces together. The final face must match image (1) only. Image (2) contributes style, not identity.

Output requirements:
- Full bleed — no white, black, or colored borders/letterboxing on any side; the image must fill the entire frame edge to edge.
- Resolution: maximum available / highest quality, sharp and detailed.
- Visual quality: rich, high-contrast, eye-catching, and beautiful overall look — the kind of image that stands out and stops the scroll, not a flat or dull result.
- Deliver in one single, final, polished result.
